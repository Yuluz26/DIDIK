# 04. WORLD 2 — BATANG LUPAR

## Theme
Dunia sungai Sarawak yang hangat dan dramatik, berasaskan mitos Bujang Senang.

## Required visual mood
- Riverbank vegetation
- Mangrove-like roots
- Wooden jetties / boardwalks
- Stilt houses / riverside village cues
- Boats
- Golden sunset light
- Reflective water
- Warm orange and green palette

## Core assets required
### A. Key art / overview
- World 2 key visual poster
- Batang Lupar chapter intro image
- World thumbnail icon
- World map preview / chapter card

### B. Auto story / transition backgrounds
- River journey background
- Dock / village passage background
- Encounter transition frame
- Chapter clear background

### C. Battle backgrounds
- Standard river encounter background
- Village-edge encounter background
- Boss battle background at sunset
- Optional storm/tide phase background

### D. Minor enemy assets
At least 3 types:
- River bandit spirit
- Crocodile scout
- Mangrove guardian

Each needs:
- concept art
- battle pose
- attack cue frame
- hurt frame
- defeated frame

### E. Boss assets — Bujang Senang
Required:
- Boss key art
- Boss reveal splash art
- Boss full battle art
- Bite attack pose
- Tail smash pose
- Water rush pose
- Damage pose
- Defeat pose
- Close-up face portrait
- Cinematic final hit frame

### F. Environmental FX
- Water slash FX
- Crocodile splash FX
- Mud burst FX
- Sunset glow overlay FX
- Fire weakness hit FX

### G. UI-specific world assets
- “Batang Lupar” title card
- “Bujang Senang” boss plate
- World clear badge
