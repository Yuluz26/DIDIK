# 03. WORLD 1 — TASIK CHINI

## Theme
Dunia tasik legenda yang indah, mistik, tenang tetapi berbahaya.

## Required visual mood
- Turquoise lake
- Lotus flowers
- Lily pads
- Wooden bridges
- Malay lakeside huts
- Ancient ruins
- Reeds, lanterns, carved stones
- Waterfall or flowing water element
- Morning mist / magical lake atmosphere

## Core assets required
### A. Key art / overview
- World 1 key visual poster
- Tasik Chini chapter intro image
- World thumbnail icon
- World map preview / chapter card

### B. Auto story / transition backgrounds
- Story travel background (wide)
- Encounter lead-in background
- Shrine / checkpoint background
- Reward / chapter clear background

### C. Battle backgrounds
- Standard encounter battle background
- Elite encounter battle background
- Boss battle background
- Alternate boss phase background (optional)

### D. Minor enemy assets
At least 3 types:
- Lake spirit / guardian imp
- Ruin watcher
- Water serpent scout

Each needs:
- concept art
- battle pose
- attack cue frame
- hurt frame
- defeated frame

### E. Boss assets — Naga Chini
Required:
- Boss key art
- Boss reveal splash art
- Boss full battle art
- Boss attack pose 1
- Boss attack pose 2
- Boss damage pose
- Boss defeat pose
- Close-up face portrait
- Cinematic final hit frame

### F. Environmental FX
- Water splash FX
- Ripple FX
- Mist FX
- Lotus glow FX
- Lightning weakness hit FX
- Water aura FX

### G. UI-specific world assets
- “Tasik Chini” title card
- “Naga Chini” boss name plate
- World clear badge
